# AI-Assisted Analytics (Concept Demo)

This document illustrates how AI/ML capabilities can augment MASINT software workflows by assisting analysts and mission leads—while maintaining governance, transparency, and human oversight.

## Purpose
- Reduce analyst cognitive load
- Prioritize anomalies and signatures for review
- Improve timeliness of mission assessments

## Example AI-Assisted Capabilities (Illustrative)
- Highlight anomalous patterns for analyst triage
- Suggest likely categories or signatures with confidence indicators
- Summarize changes over time to support rapid assessment

## Integration into MASINT Software Workflow
- AI services operate as **decision-support**, not decision-makers
- Outputs are surfaced alongside provenance and confidence
- Analysts retain full authority over assessments and actions

## Governance and Guardrails
- Intended use and limitations clearly documented
- Evaluation criteria defined prior to deployment
- Monitoring for drift, failure modes, and unexpected behavior
- Human-in-the-loop oversight for mission decisions

This concept aligns with responsible AI adoption in IC and defense environments.
