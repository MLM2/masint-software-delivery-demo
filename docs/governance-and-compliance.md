# Governance and Compliance (Illustrative)

This document outlines governance and compliance considerations for MASINT-aligned software development in Intelligence Community environments.

## Delivery Governance
- Defined delivery milestones and release gates
- Stakeholder review and approval checkpoints
- Change control and configuration management
- Clear ownership of decisions and trade-offs

## Security and DevSecOps Alignment
- Security requirements captured as backlog items
- Integration with CI/CD security checks (conceptual)
- Role-based access control and audit logging
- Documentation to support accreditation and sustainment

## Risk Management
- Maintain a program-level risk register
- Identify and mitigate integration, performance, and dependency risks
- Track issues and corrective actions to closure
- Escalate risks based on mission impact

## Compliance Considerations
- Alignment with DIA and IC architecture guidance
- Configuration baselines and version control
- Traceability from requirements to delivery
- Readiness for audits, reviews, and sustainment handoff

## Responsible Use of AI (When Applicable)
If AI/ML capabilities are integrated:
- Define intended use and operational boundaries
- Establish evaluation and monitoring criteria
- Document limitations and risks
- Maintain human oversight for mission decisions
