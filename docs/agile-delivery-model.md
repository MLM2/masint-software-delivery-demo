# Agile Delivery Model (TPM Playbook)

This document describes an illustrative Agile delivery model for MASINT-aligned software development, from the perspective of a Technical Program Manager (TPM).

## Operating Cadence
- Two-week sprint cadence
- Weekly mission and technical stakeholder sync
- Monthly release readiness and risk review
- Quarterly roadmap and planning refresh (PI Planning when applicable)

## Planning Artifacts
- Product vision and high-level roadmap
- Epics, features, and mission user stories
- Dependency mapping across teams and systems
- Risk register with mitigation actions and owners

## Agile Ceremonies
- Sprint planning
- Daily stand-up
- Backlog refinement
- Sprint review and demo to mission stakeholders
- Retrospective
- Program Increment (PI) planning for multi-team efforts

## TPM Responsibilities
- Translate mission needs and analytic workflows into actionable technical requirements
- Manage cost, schedule, and performance reporting
- Track risks, blockers, and cross-team dependencies
- Drive prioritization decisions and trade-offs with stakeholders
- Ensure delivery aligns with security, governance, and compliance constraints

## Delivery Outcomes
- Predictable and transparent delivery
- Incremental capability releases aligned to mission needs
- Early identification of integration and performance risks
- Clear communication to government sponsors and technical leadership
